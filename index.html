AI Trading Signals and Trading Bots for Every Market

SignalBots reads seven markets: forex, binary options, crypto, gold and silver, oil and gas, the indices and CME futures, and turns what it finds into trade plans you can act on: a side, an entry, a stop-loss and take-profit levels, fixed before the call is published. The AI trading signals reach you wherever you already trade, through a browser extension, a Telegram channel, MetaTrader 4 and 5, TradingView alerts, the mobile apps or the web dashboard, and the trading bots act on them for you on supported brokers. You keep your own broker and your own account, and every position stays your decision.

Live Trading Signals With Entry, Stop-Loss and Take-Profit

Every live trading signal on the site is the same shape, whichever market it came from: a BUY or SELL, the instrument, the entry, the stop-loss and the take-profit levels, with the structure and volatility context that led to them. Auto-trading bots

Auto Trading Bots That Run Inside Your Broker's Own Tab

The auto trading bots are browser extensions for Chrome, Firefox and Edge, and they work where you already trade rather than in a separate app. Each binary broker has its own bot, Pocket Option, Quotex, IQ Option and Olymp Trade, with a separate weekend OTC bot for each, and it reads the chart in your broker tab and can place the trade on your own account. Alongside them sit broker-neutral market bots for forex, gold and silver, oil, the indices, US stocks, crypto and binary options, which signal rather than trade. Binary options carry the risk of losing the whole stake, so set your own limits before you switch a bot on.

Telegram channels

Telegram Trading Signals, One VIP Channel per Market

If you would rather read the calls than open a dashboard, the Telegram trading signals are split into VIP channels, one per market, so you join only what you trade: forex, crypto, gold and silver, oil and gas, the indices and CME futures. Binary options have their own channels, picked by broker instead of by market, with a weekday and a weekend OTC channel each. MT4/MT5 robots

MT5 Expert Advisors That Trade Smart Money Setups

The ICT AI Signal expert advisor runs inside MetaTrader 5 and reads the chart the way the methodology does: market structure first, then the liquidity sweep, the order block price returned to and the fair value gap it left behind, before a CHoCH confirms an entry, a stop-loss and two take-profit levels. It runs on forex and CFD brokers, on crypto exchanges through the MT5 connector, and on prop-firm evaluation accounts, and it trades on your own terminal and your own account. An expert advisor automates the execution, never the risk: set the lot size and the limits yourself.

MT5 connectors

MT5 Connectors That Bring Your Exchange Chart Into MetaTrader

A connector puts a feed MetaTrader does not carry onto an MT5 chart. Download the .ex5 for your venue, load it in the terminal, and the exchange's own prices are drawn as a normal chart, so your indicators, your templates and the expert advisor read it like any other symbol. There are connectors for eleven crypto exchanges, Binance, Bybit and OKX among them, and for the binary brokers' weekend OTC pairs, which exist nowhere else. It carries the chart and the signals into the terminal; the account, the venue and the execution stay exactly where they already are.

TradingView

TradingView Indicators and Smart Money Scanners

If your chart work already lives in TradingView, the indicators run there instead of asking you to move. The space carries Smart Money and ICT scanners (liquidity sweep, supply and demand, the ICT liquidity pool and the price-action screener) plus binary options tools, a synthetic-indices spike detector and cross-market screeners for correlation, trend and divergence. A screener watches a whole list and marks only what is worth reading, so you open two charts instead of twenty, and a webhook alert can hand the confirmed setup on to Telegram, the MT5 connector or the apps. Access is arranged with the support desk.

Our engine fires a signal in under 10 ms and pushes it straight to Telegram, email, browser and the EA at the same moment, so manual and automated traders act on the exact same entry, with no relay lag.

No. We never hold, access or withdraw your funds. Signals and the EA run inside your own broker account. You keep full control and can switch automation off at any time.

Yes. The same signal you can act on by hand (Telegram, on-site feed, mobile push) also drives the EA and browser extension when you want hands-off execution. One engine, your choice of manual or automated.

Forex, Binary Options, Crypto, Gold, Indices and Futures, across the major sessions. Each market has its own dedicated feed and Telegram channel so you only get the setups you actually trade.

How reliable are the signals?

Each feed shows its historical win rate and reward-to-risk ratio from real, backtested results, no profit promises. Trading carries risk; review our risk warning before you start.

Is it beginner-friendly?

Yes. Each signal arrives as a ready-made plan (entry, stop-loss and target), so you don't need to read charts to act. Start by watching the free feed, then follow along when you feel ready.

How many signals will I get?

It depends on the market and volatility: the engine only fires when a setup meets its criteria, so you get quality over noise. Active markets typically see several clean setups per session.

Where do I receive the signals?

Wherever you already trade: Telegram and browser push on every plan, plus the web dashboard and mobile app. The EA and browser extension act on the same signals in your trading platform.
